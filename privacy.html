Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy applies to AdMob Daily Report and its separate authorized Google Apps Script setups maintained by Jeet Patel.

Data accessed

The tool accesses the authorized AdMob publisher ID, reporting timezone, currency, app identifiers and names, and estimated earnings. A destination email address is configured by the account operator.

Purpose and sharing

Data is used only to generate daily earnings summaries and email them to the configured recipient. Google services process the data to run the script and deliver the email. The recipient's email provider processes the delivered report.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Reports are not published on this website.

Storage and retention

Publisher IDs and recipient addresses are stored in the private script configuration until changed or deleted. Report data is processed during execution and retained in delivered emails according to the relevant mailbox settings. Execution logs may contain report dates, status messages and API error information, subject to Google's logging retention.

Google manages authorization. OAuth tokens and account passwords are not placed in this public website or its repository.

Control and deletion

Operators can stop scheduled reports by deleting the Apps Script trigger and revoke access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Script configuration can be removed by deleting the script. Existing emails must be deleted separately from the mailboxes that hold them.

Website hosting

This information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. No analytics or advertising scripts are added by the maintainer. GitHub may process technical access information under its own privacy policy.

Google API data

AdMob Daily Report's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and updates

Contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile. Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised date.

Terms of Service

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Purpose

AdMob Daily Report is a private reporting utility maintained by Jeet Patel. It emails estimated earnings summaries from authorized AdMob accounts.

Authorized use

Use the tool only with accounts you own or are authorized to access. Configure report recipients only where you have permission to share the account's reporting information.

Accuracy and availability

Earnings are estimates supplied by Google and may change. Reports are not payment statements. Delivery depends on Google services, valid authorization, quotas and email delivery. Exact delivery times and uninterrupted operation are not guaranteed.

Stopping use

You can stop using the tool by deleting its scheduled trigger and revoking its Google account access.

Privacy and contact

Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy. Contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile.

This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
